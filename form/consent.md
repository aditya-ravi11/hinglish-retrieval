## Hinglish search queries: help build a test set

This is a student research project on search in Hinglish, meaning Hindi typed in English letters ("mujhe sasta phone chahiye").

**What you do.** You see one question at a time. Type it the way you would type it to a friend or in a search bar. There is no right or wrong spelling. Type the way you normally do. You can skip any question and stop whenever you like.

**What is saved.** Only what you type, which question it was for, and timestamps. Your browser gets a random id so you never see the same question twice. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or IP address. The two optional questions below are not linked to who you are.

**Please do not type** names, phone numbers, or anything personal into the answers.

**How it will be used.** Your answers will be published as part of a free public dataset for research, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), the same licence as the Wikipedia text the questions come from. Answers are anonymous, so they cannot be removed one by one after publication.

By pressing "I agree, start" you confirm you are 18 or older and agree to this.
